Not just the internet you use. The one you build.

Work from fill-in-the-blanks starter templates and strict OpenAPI 3.0 specifications. Bring four separate services to life, then connect them into a functioning system.

The work

Build the route from a web request to a verified session.

Your client asks DNS where the authentication service lives. The server handles a login or registration fallback, the database stores credentials, and a session cookie proves who signed in.

The learning

Make the invisible parts of networking visible.

Explore Linux, IP addresses, ports, TCP and UDP, web development, backend services, and service discovery while solving realistic failure cases as a team.

You do not need to know everything beforehand. Problem-solving, teamwork, and adaptability matter most.

Four services form one system.

Each teammate owns one service. The contracts keep the pieces compatible while your team works independently and integrates at the end.

acm-dns
